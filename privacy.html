ShortsStudio

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Introduction

ShortsStudio is an independent desktop application for short-form video production, social media publishing and performance analysis.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when using ShortsStudio and its authorized integrations.

2. Data Controller

ShortsStudio is operated by Enrique Sanclemente Planes, based in Spain.

Privacy inquiries may be sent to: phreakphantom99@proton.me .

3. Information Processed

Depending on the features enabled and permissions granted, ShortsStudio may process:

  • Social media account identifiers and profile information.
  • OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens.
  • Video files, captions, titles, descriptions and hashtags.
  • Publication identifiers, timestamps and status information.
  • Available video and account performance metrics.
  • Locally generated configuration files and application logs.

Social media data is accessed only through authorized integrations and within the permissions granted by the account holder.

4. How Information Is Used

Information is processed to:

  • Authenticate authorized social media accounts.
  • Create and manage video production workflows.
  • Upload and publish content when authorized.
  • Retrieve publication status and performance metrics.
  • Prevent duplicate publications.
  • Maintain local publication records and historical statistics.
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve reliability.

5. TikTok Integration

When the TikTok integration becomes available, ShortsStudio may access account information, video metadata and available performance metrics through authorized TikTok APIs.

Video files and associated metadata may be transferred to TikTok when the account holder initiates an authorized upload or publication workflow.

Access depends on the permissions granted by the user and the approval status of the application.

6. YouTube Integration

ShortsStudio uses YouTube API Services to support authorized video uploads, publication management and retrieval of available video information.

Users can review Google's Privacy Policy at: https://policies.google.com/privacy .

Google account permissions can be managed or revoked at: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions .

7. Instagram Integration

When available, the Instagram integration will process authorized professional account information, media publication data and available insights through Meta APIs.

8. Local Storage and Security

The current desktop implementation stores configuration, publication records and authorization credentials locally on the user's device.

Authorization credentials are kept separately from the application source code and excluded from the source control repository.

Users are responsible for protecting access to their devices and local credential files.

9. Third-Party Services

ShortsStudio communicates with third-party platforms when authorized features are used.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • TikTok Privacy Policy
  • Meta Privacy Policy

ShortsStudio does not sell personal information or use social media account data for advertising.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Local information is retained as needed for the application's functionality and until it is deleted by the user or no longer required.

Users may revoke platform authorization through their social media account settings and remove locally stored credentials, publication records and other application data.

Data deletion requests can also be submitted using the contact email provided in this policy.

11. Legal Basis and User Rights

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, personal data is processed on an appropriate legal basis, including consent for optional integrations and legitimate interests in maintaining application functionality and security, where applicable.

Users may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing, request data portability, withdraw consent and lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority.

12. Children's Privacy

ShortsStudio is intended for adult content creators and is not directed at children.

13. Website Hosting

This informational website is hosted through GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, according to its own privacy practices.

This website does not intentionally use advertising trackers or analytics cookies.

14. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as application functionality and integrations evolve. The latest version will be available on this page.

15. Contact

For privacy questions or data-related requests, contact: phreakphantom99@proton.me .
